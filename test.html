1. Site Name

Site Name: Paws & Play

Reason for choosing this name:

The name Paws & Play instantly conveys warmth, joy, and active companionship—three core emotions associated with dog ownership. It is short, memorable, and easy to spell, making it ideal for word-of-mouth sharing. The word "Paws" anchors the site firmly in the pet/dog niche, while "Play" suggests a fun, approachable, and engaging community hub rather than a sterile, clinical resource. It perfectly bridges the gap between adoption awareness (serious mission) and daily care tips (lighthearted and practical).

2. Site Purpose

Paws & Play serves as a welcoming digital community hub for current and aspiring dog owners. The site provides three core services:

  • Adoption Discovery: Showcasing popular, adoptable dog breeds with key traits to help potential owners find their perfect match.
  • Practical Care Education: Offering bite-sized, beginner-friendly guides on feeding, grooming, and basic training to build confidence in new pet parents.
  • Local Connection: Providing information on local adoption centers, operating hours, and a direct contact channel for inquiries.

The overarching goal is to reduce pet surrender rates by ensuring that every new dog owner feels prepared, supported, and connected to a caring community.

3. Scenarios (Target Audience Questions)

The following questions reflect real concerns from our primary audience—first-time dog owners and families considering adoption:

Scenario 1
Question: "I've never owned a dog before. What are the absolute basics for feeding, grooming, and training a puppy?"
Answered on: The Care Guides page, where three clear cards break down Feeding 101, Grooming Basics, and Training Tips into digestible steps.
Scenario 2
Question: "Which dog breed is best for a small apartment, and where can I go to adopt one locally?"
Answered on: The Home page (breed cards with traits like size and energy level) and the About & Contact page (which lists local adoption center hours and locations).

4. Color Schema

I have selected a warm, inviting, and trustworthy palette. The table below shows each color, its hex code, and its exact application across the site. (These colors are applied to this very document to demonstrate the theme.)

Role Hex Code Visual Swatch Where It Will Be Used
Primary Brand (Blue) #2A5C7A Navigation bar background, main headings (H1, H2), footer background, primary button backgrounds, hyperlink hover states.
Secondary Brand (Gold) #F4A261 Accent elements (active nav link underline), card borders, interactive icons (like the "Favorite" heart), and call-to-action highlights.
Background (Cream) #FFFDF7 Main page background, card backgrounds, and input fields to create a soft, clean, and readable canvas.
Body Text #2D3E3A All paragraphs, list items, and general body copy to ensure high readability with low eye-strain.

5. Typography

To balance friendliness with readability, I have selected exactly two Google Fonts. Their applications are outlined below:

Font Name Category Weights Used Where It Will Be Applied
Nunito Sans-Serif (Rounded) 400, 600, 800 Primary Body Font: Used for all navigation links, paragraphs, card descriptions, lists, and form labels. The rounded terminals give it a friendly, approachable feel that matches the pet niche. Regular 400 · Semi-Bold 600 · Extra-Bold 800
Playfair Display Serif (High Contrast) 700 (Bold) Headings & Titles: Used exclusively for H1, H2, and H3 headings (e.g., "Find Your Furry Friend," "Care Guides"). The elegant contrast of this serif against the rounded sans-serif creates a premium, trustworthy editorial feel for the main titles. Playfair Display 700 — Bold
