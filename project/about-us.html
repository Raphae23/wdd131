🏡 About Ralph Pet Store

Ralph Pet Store was founded by Maya, a lifelong dog lover and rescue advocate. After adopting her first dog, Bailey, from a local shelter, Maya realized that many new pet owners feel overwhelmed and unprepared. She built this site to offer clear, compassionate guidance - so every dog finds a loving, confident home.

We believe that adoption saves lives, and education keeps families together. Every breed featured here is commonly found in shelters; we encourage you to adopt, not shop.

Our mission is to reduce pet surrender rates by ensuring that every new dog owner feels prepared, supported, and connected to a caring community.
